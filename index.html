ABOUT REHABX-JEYPORE

Physiotherapy that focuses on you.

At RehabX-Jeypore, we believe quality physiotherapy is not just about treating pain—it is about helping people move better, live independently, and return to the activities they love.

Our approach starts by understanding your symptoms, lifestyle, physical condition and goals. Each patient receives an individualized assessment and treatment plan aimed at addressing the underlying problem, not simply managing symptoms.
